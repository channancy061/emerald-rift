Starting a new game
When you start a new game you're asked a few questions. Read each one — don't keep pressing A! Yes is picked first; press B to answer No. You can't change your answers later in that save.
  • Difficulty: you choose Easy, Normal, Hard or Extra Hard. It changes the important trainers' teams (see each battle below).
  • • Easy: only important battles stop you. Other trainers battle only if you talk to them.
  • • Normal: trainers behave as in the original game.
  • • Hard: every trainer spots you from far away and in every direction, so you can't sneak past. A Gym Leader only battles you once every trainer in their Gym is beaten.
  • • Extra Hard: as Hard, and every trainer has six Pokémon, one level higher.
  • EVs and IVs off: you choose. Say yes and every Pokémon has perfect stats from the start - no training needed.
  • Hard level cap: you choose. Say yes and your Pokémon stop at the cap for the next important battle.
  • No items in battle: you choose. Say yes and you can't use your bag in trainer battles.
  • Infinite Candy: you choose. Say yes to get a Rare Candy that never runs out (it stops at the level cap).
  • Adaptive bosses: you choose. Say yes and gym leaders, your rival and the Elite Four swap one Pokémon to counter your team (marked ? below).
  • Nuzlocke: you choose. If you say yes, these rules follow:
  • • Only the first wild Pokémon you meet in each area can be caught, counting from when you have Poké Balls. In a wild double battle, knock one out and catch the other.
  • • A Pokémon that faints is gone for good - no revives.
  • • If your whole team faints, you choose at the start: go on with healthy Pokémon from your PC, or the run is over. No battle starts while nobody in your party can fight.
  • • Duplicates clause: a Pokémon you already caught (or its evolution family) doesn't use up the area. (you choose)
  • • Eevee clause: Eevee never counts as a duplicate. (you choose)
  • • Shiny clause: shiny Pokémon can always be caught. (you choose)
  • • Static encounters count: legendaries and Pokémon you walk up to use up the area too. Saying yes asks you again, because a legendary could be lost. (you choose)
  • • Every Pokémon you catch needs a nickname (not its species name). (you choose)
  • • The level cap is on. (you choose)
  • In every Pokémon Center, the helper can change a Pokémon's nature (¥2,000), give perfect IVs (free), set its EVs (free).

After the Champion

Becoming Champion isn't the end. Kanto and Hoenn are still collapsing into each other, and the credits wait until that's stopped.

  • After the Hall of Fame, Steven is waiting at home and asks you to meet at Cerulean Cave.
  • There Steven battles you first (see the team above), above the Champion's level. The level cap goes up to match.
  • Win and Steven takes you to Birth Island. You can heal and change your team before you say you're ready.
  • Then Deoxys's 4 forms attack one after another, at level 93, with no healing in between. Their order and moves are chosen against your team, so every form brings moves that hit you hard. Bring Pokémon that cover each other's weaknesses.
  • After the last form, Deoxys (level 90) has to be caught to stop the collapse. Bring plenty of Poké Balls. If it gets away, talk to Steven to try again.
  • If your team falls to Deoxys: without the Nuzlocke, try again from Steven; in a Nuzlocke, the run is over.
  • Once it's caught, the regions separate, the visiting challengers go home, and the credits roll.
